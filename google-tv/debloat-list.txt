# Known Google TV / Android TV packages and how risky they are to disable.
# Format: risk|package|what it is|what stops working if disabled
#
# risk levels:
#   low    - extras almost nobody needs; pre-selected in the plan
#   app    - ordinary apps; only disable if you don't use them (not pre-selected)
#   medium - removes a feature some people rely on (not pre-selected)
#   keep   - core system; gtv.sh refuses to disable these
#
# Package identifications are best-effort; names can vary by device and firmware.
# Only packages actually installed on your TV are shown in the plan.
